How it works

Three taps from the sofa to a new friend.

  1. 📍

    Find a meetup

    Open the map. Every pin is something happening near you: a walk, a run, a coffee.

  2. 🙌

    Tap I'm in

    One tap and you're on the list. You can see who else is coming. Can't go? Tap I can't go now.

  3. 🤘

    Turn up

    Meet in public, look for the faces from the app, and get going. After your first meetup you can post your own.

House rules

Simple, and the same for everyone.

💎Everyone is Diamond+ Everyone is treated equally in this app.

  • 18+ only.
  • Be kind.
  • Meet in public.
  • A photo of your face, so people know who to look for.
  • No selling or advertising.
  • No means no.
  • 🚩 3 reports = banned for life.

Questions

Tap one.

What is Packmates?

A free map of meetups. Pick one. Turn up. Nobody can message you.

Who is it for?

Everyone. Anyone 18 or over can join, and the host can choose an age range for their meetup.

How do I go to a meetup?

Tap a pin on the Map. Tap I'm in. Turn up. Can't go? Tap I can't go now.

How do I post a meetup?

Tap Post and answer a few questions. You can post after your first meetup.

How do I stay safe?

Stay in public. Tell a friend where you are. Feels wrong? Leave.

Someone was rude

Tap 🚩 Report or block under their name. Blocking hides you from each other.
